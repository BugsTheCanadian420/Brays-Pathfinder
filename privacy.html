Privacy

Pathfinder is a fan-made Destiny 2 companion. It is not made by or affiliated with Bungie.

Signing in

You sign in on Bungie.net. Pathfinder never sees your Bungie password. The sign-in keys Bungie gives back are kept on your own device: encrypted by Windows in the PC app, and in your browser's storage on the website. They are exchanged through Pathfinder's small sign-in service on Cloudflare, which holds the app's own Bungie secret so it never has to be in the app. That service does not keep your keys.

Your Destiny data

Your characters, inventory, triumphs and activity history are read from Bungie's official API while you use the app, and kept on your device to load faster next time. Items are only moved, equipped or locked when you ask.

Sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync, your loadouts, tags, notes, settings and wish lists are stored on Cloudflare under your Bungie.net account number, so your PC and phone match. Only a signed-in session for that account can read or change them. Turn Sync off to stop sending them.

Share links

When you make a share link, the loadout, its name, a short summary of the gear and a preview picture are stored so anyone with the link can open it. Your Bungie name is not included. Share links do not expire at the moment; to have one removed, ask on the project page below.

What Pathfinder does not do

  • No ads, and nothing is sold.
  • No tracking cookies or advertising trackers.
  • The problem log in Settings stays on your device unless you copy it and send it yourself.

Cloudflare, which hosts the website and the sign-in service, keeps its own standard request logs. Bungie's handling of your account is covered by Bungie's privacy policy.
