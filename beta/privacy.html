Signing in with Bungie

You sign in on Bungie.net itself, so Pathfinder never sees your Bungie password. Bungie hands back sign-in keys, which stay on your device: encrypted by Windows in the PC app, and in your browser's storage on the website.

The keys are exchanged through Pathfinder's small sign-in service on Cloudflare. It holds Pathfinder's own Bungie app secret, so that secret never has to ship inside the app. It passes your keys along and does not keep them.

Your Destiny data

Your characters, inventory, triumphs and activity history are read from Bungie's official API while you use Pathfinder, and a copy is kept on your device so it opens faster next time. Items are only moved, equipped, locked or changed when you ask.

Tags, notes, loadouts, wish lists and settings live on your device too, unless you turn on Sync.

Pathfinder accounts (optional)

A Pathfinder account is an account name and a password that bring all your Bungie accounts to every device you use. Everything works without one.

Your password
Never leaves your device. It is turned into two keys there: one proves it is you, the other locks your data.
Your Bungie sign-ins
Locked on your device with your password before they are sent. Our service stores only the locked copy and cannot open it.
What we store
Your account name, a one-way check of your password, when the account was made and last used, and whether it is a player, developer or founder account.

If you forget your password, Pathfinder's owner can give you a one-time reset code, or set a temporary password that you have to change when you sign in. Neither lets anyone see your password. Because your saved sign-ins were locked with the old password, they are cleared and saved again from your device. The owner and developers can also rename an account whose name breaks the rules; that resets the password the same way.

You can sign out on one device or on all of them, and delete your account at any time in Settings > Accounts.

Sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync, your loadouts, tags, notes, settings, tracked triumphs and wish lists are stored on Cloudflare under your Bungie.net account number, so your PC and phone match. Only a signed-in session for that account can read or change them. Turn Sync off to stop sending them.

Share links

When you make a share link, the loadout, its name, a short summary of the gear and a preview picture are stored so anyone with the link can open it. Your Bungie name is not shown. Your account number is stored with it, so sharing the same loadout again gives the same link, but it is never shown to anyone. Share links do not expire at the moment; to have one removed, email us.

Community perk ratings

The perk percentages in item pop-ups come from two places:

  • Public games. Every ten minutes Pathfinder reads a few recent public post-game reports from Bungie, then the weapons those players have equipped, which Bungie makes public for every account. Only weapon and perk numbers are counted. To count each player at most once a week, their Destiny account number and the time they were counted are kept.
  • Pathfinder players who opt in. "Share my kept rolls anonymously" is off unless you turn it on. It sends only the weapon and perk numbers of weapons you keep, nothing about you or your account. A scrambled form of your internet address is kept for the day, only to cap how much one address can send.

Help requests

When you ask for help in Settings > Help, the Pathfinder team receives your message, the kind you picked (bug, idea, question), the Discord name or email you typed (if any), your Pathfinder version and whether you used the PC app or the website. If you are signed in to a Pathfinder account, its name comes too, so the request shows on all your devices. If you leave "Attach the problem report" ticked, the errors Pathfinder logged on your device, your browser version and screen size come with it; nothing about your Destiny account.

The team's answers are stored with your request so you can read them in the app. Without a Pathfinder account, a secret key saved on your device is what lets you open your requests. Requests are only read by the Pathfinder team (its owner and developers) and are deleted 180 days after the last message, or sooner if the team deletes them. A scrambled form of your internet address is kept for the day, only to cap how many requests one address can open.

Services we use

  • Bungie: your Destiny data and sign-in. Covered by Bungie's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and Pathfinder's services, and keeps its own standard request logs.
  • GitHub: hosts the PC app downloads.
  • Google Fonts: serves some of the typefaces, so Google sees a request from your browser like any website would.
  • Sites in the Hub tab (light.gg, DIM and others) are run by other people under their own policies, and open only when you choose them.

What we never do

  • Show ads, or sell or rent any data.
  • Use tracking cookies or advertising trackers.
  • Move, delete or change your items without you asking.
  • Send the problem log in Settings anywhere. It stays on your device unless you copy it and send it yourself.

Removing your data

  • On your device: sign out in Settings > Accounts, or clear the app's or site's data.
  • Pathfinder account: Settings > Accounts > Delete Pathfinder account removes it and the sign-ins saved in it.
  • Help requests, Sync, share links or anything else: email us and we will remove it.
